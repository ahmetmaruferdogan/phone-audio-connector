# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are listed here.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-02

- Releases now include `SHA256SUMS.txt` so you can check your download is genuine.
- README: clearer download steps, SmartScreen warning help, and how to spot fake copies.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

First release.

- Plays phone audio on the PC over Bluetooth (A2DP sink).
- Keeps ticked devices connected and reconnects after out of range, Bluetooth off/on, and sleep.
- Works when connecting from the phone.
- Tray icon, "Start with Windows", light and dark theme.
- Logs hide Bluetooth addresses and device names.
